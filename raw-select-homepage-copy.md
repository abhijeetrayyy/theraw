# Raw Select — Homepage Copy & Content Plan

## Brand Positioning Summary

**Raw Select** is a curated material and design solutions platform for architects and interior designers. The core philosophy: clarity over excess, refined selection over abundance. The brand speaks to professionals who are tired of endless catalogs and want a trusted, pre-vetted selection of materials that meet the highest standards.

**Tone of voice:** Confident, precise, minimal, authoritative. Not salesy. Speaks like a knowledgeable peer, not a vendor. Short sentences. Long pauses. Let the words breathe.

**Target audience:** Architects, interior designers, specifiers — professionals who value quality over quantity and want a partner that does the filtering for them.

## CONTENT NOTES FOR DESIGNERS & DEVELOPERS

### Imagery Direction
- **Hero:** Full-bleed, slow-motion video or large-format photography of a single, striking material surface (e.g., veined marble, brushed metal, raw concrete). Minimal. No people. Let the material be the subject.
- **Collection:** Clean, well-lit product photography. Consistent background. Materials shown at scale with context (e.g., a slab in a space, not floating).
- **Projects:** Editorial-quality interior photography. Spaces that feel lived-in, not staged. Focus on material details — close-ups of surfaces, textures, junctions.
- **Process:** Behind-the-scenes imagery. Hands selecting samples. Workshop environments. Authentic, not polished.
- **Philosophy:** Abstract or atmospheric. Light on texture. Space and silence.

### Typography Direction
- **Headlines:** Thin, wide-tracked sans-serif or elegant serif. Large scale. Plenty of whitespace.
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif. Generous line height (1.6–1.8). Comfortable measure (60–75 characters).
- **Labels/UI:** Small, uppercase, wide-tracked. Functional, not decorative.

### Color Direction
- **Primary:** Near-black (#0A0A0A or similar)
- **Secondary:** Warm off-white (#F5F5F0 or similar)
- **Accent:** Single, restrained accent color (e.g., deep blue, muted gold, or terracotta — depending on brand personality)
- **Text:** High contrast for body. Muted for secondary information.

### Animation Direction
- **Scroll reveals:** Elements fade in + translate up (20–40px) as they enter viewport. Staggered for lists/grids.
- **Horizontal sections:** Vertical scroll drives horizontal movement. Smooth, not jerky.
- **Parallax:** Subtle. Background elements move slower than foreground. Max 10–15% differential.
- **Hover:** Minimal. Slight scale or opacity change. Never distracting.
- **Page transitions:** Fade through black. 400–600ms. Feels deliberate, not rushed.
- **Text reveals:** Words or lines appear sequentially. Typewriter or fade-in. Used sparingly — only for key headlines.

### Accessibility
- All animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Color contrast meets WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for body text)
- All images have descriptive alt text
- Navigation is keyboard-accessible
- Form fields have visible labels and error states
